# Outreach Drafts

One quoted sentence from one practitioner is worth more to this submission than any feature built in the same week. These are ready to send; personalize the greeting, send from your own address, and ask nothing that takes the recipient more than five minutes.

## 1. PlanetRead / Brij Kothari (the founder of the field)

To: contact@planetread.org (and via the contact form at planetread.org)
Subject: A working karaoke-subtitle reader and Fire TV app, built on your SLS research

Dear Professor Kothari and the PlanetRead team,

Your Same Language Subtitling research is the entire evidence base behind a project I have built for the Amazon Developer Hackathon: EveryWord, word-by-word highlighted captions for TV and web, generated automatically for any video or audio with word-level speech recognition.

You can try it in one click, no install: https://d34emfdcezeszz.cloudfront.net (a public-domain Aesop fable; press Play and the words light as they are spoken). The same renderer runs on a Fire TV app, and the caption engine, format, and renderer are open source under MIT: https://github.com/usv240/everyword. Measured timing accuracy against gold alignments is 30 ms median, documented in the repository.

Two small asks, either or both:

1. One sentence of reaction I may quote in the hackathon submission, with attribution as you prefer.
2. Anything you think is wrong or missing, which I would value even more.

Everything about this project credits SLS and links to your research. Thank you for twenty-five years of proving this works.

Ujwal Vanjare
https://github.com/usv240/everyword

## 2. Turn On The Subtitles campaign

To: via us.turnonthesubtitles.org contact
Subject: A working implementation of what TOTS asks platforms for, plus the karaoke version

Hello TOTS team,

Your campaign asks platforms to turn on same-language subtitles. I have built the stronger version of that ask as a working product for the Amazon Developer Hackathon: EveryWord, subtitles where each word lights up as it is spoken (the karaoke SLS variant from the Indian research), running on the web and on a Fire TV app, generated automatically for any content.

Try it in ten seconds: https://d34emfdcezeszz.cloudfront.net. It is open source (MIT), the caption format is an open spec, and timing accuracy is measured at 30 ms median against gold alignments: https://github.com/usv240/everyword.

If it would be useful to the campaign as a demonstration that this is technically easy for platforms now, please use it. And if anyone on the team would give me one quotable sentence of reaction for the hackathon submission, I would be grateful.

Ujwal Vanjare
