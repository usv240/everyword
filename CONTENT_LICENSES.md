Two caption pipelines, one open format. For human-narrated audio, Amazon Transcribe supplies word timings (measured at 30 ms median onset error against gold alignments; see docs/EVAL.md). For synthesized narration, Amazon Polly speech marks supply both the timings and the words, so those captions reproduce the source text exactly, punctuation included, with zero word error by construction.

Captions carry the same license as this repository (MIT) to the extent they are protectable at all; the underlying texts and the LibriVox recording are public domain.
