Subtitles that light up word by word as they are spoken. The same technique that has given reading practice to 200 million people in India, running here on a real public-domain story with captions generated by the EveryWord pipeline on Amazon Transcribe.

Measured, not promised: against gold-standard word alignments on LibriSpeech (27 speakers, 788 words we did not record), the highlight lands within 30 ms of the spoken word (median), never lights a word early beyond 150 ms in 766 matched words, and breaks lines at real pauses 42 percent of the time where timing-blind chunking manages 2.
